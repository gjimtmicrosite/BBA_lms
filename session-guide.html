3 Hours · BBA Sem 1
Time BlockFaculty ActionStudent Activity

  • Introduce Reliance Retail market study project and expected outcomes.
  • Demonstrate how to use ChatGPT and Copilot for market research.

  • Guide students in generating store databases using AI.

  • Review student findings.
  • Facilitate discussion on expansion opportunities.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab Work
  • Verify store locations using online sources and AI assistance.
  • Use ChatGPT and Copilot to standardize location details.
  • Categorize stores into residential, commercial, and mixed-use areas.
  • Use Excel AI to organize and analyze store coverage.
  • Generate AI-assisted market gap observations.
Hr 3 · 2:00–3:00
✅ Store Format & Customer Mapping
  • Demonstrate customer profiling using AI.
  • Explain catchment area analysis using ChatGPT and Copilot.

  • Prepare AI-generated summary of market presence findings.
